Three languages, two directions

Layouts use logical start / end

FR Jeu de plaquettes de frein avant — en stock 4 850 DA
AR طقم وسادات الفرامل الأمامية — متوفر 4 850 دج

Arabic mirrors the shell: sidebar on the right, money columns stay end-aligned. Amounts and part references are isolated LTR runs (bdi dir=ltr), so 4 850 never reads as 850 4.
